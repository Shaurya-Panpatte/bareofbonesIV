GAME GUIDE

How every stat and upgrade works

No hidden player-order combat buff

Every player starts with the same army, efficiency, technology, armor, logistics, fortification, morale, money, and purchased-upgrade levels. The procedural map can still differ slightly by tile count, terrain mix, capital terrain, and turn order.

Upgrade shop

Army

Adds troops immediately. The first purchase adds 24 troops; later purchases give 2 fewer each time, down to a minimum of 12. Large armies cost more upkeep and can suffer supply attrition.

Starting cost: $140 · Cost rises by $80 per purchase

Efficiency

Adds 5 efficiency, up to 120. Efficiency raises round income and changes combat strength by 0.25% for every point above or below 85.

Starting cost: $170 · Cost rises by $90 per purchase

Technology

Adds 1 technology level, up to level 10. Every level above 2 adds 3.5% combat strength and also improves automatic recruitment each round.

Starting cost: $260 · Cost rises by $165 per purchase

Armor

Adds 1 armored vehicle. Armor increases defense with diminishing returns, capped at +12%. Each vehicle costs $7 upkeep per round and can be destroyed in the interception minigame.

Starting cost: $220 · Cost rises by $125 per purchase

Logistics

Adds 1 level, up to level 8. Each level above 1 adds 1.2% attack strength, raises the local defending force by 1 percentage point, adds about 2.2 recruits per round, and supports 28 more troops before attrition.

Starting cost: $200 · Cost rises by $135 per purchase

Fortification

Adds 1 level, up to level 8. Each level above 1 adds 1.8% defense to every territory you currently defend.

Starting cost: $170 · Cost rises by $100 per purchase

Combat modifiers

MoraleEach point above or below 100 changes combat strength by 0.3%. Morale rises after victories and falls after defeats.
War exhaustionEach point reduces combat strength by 0.15%. It rises after fighting and falls by 8 each new round.
Friendly supportEach friendly tile adjacent to the attacking source tile adds 1.2% attack strength.
Home defenseDefenders receive a fixed +3% bonus.
CapitalDefending your original capital adds +8% defense.
Local defense forceA territory uses 22% of the defender’s total army, plus 1 percentage point for every logistics level above 1, capped at 29%.

Terrain and battle estimates

PlainsNo terrain defense bonus.
Forest+6% defense.
Mountain+12% defense.
Bridge+8% defense and a −4% attack penalty for the attacker.

The displayed estimate combines committed troops, the defender’s local force, stats, terrain, armor, fortification, morale, exhaustion, support, logistics, and capital status. Estimates are capped between 15% and 85%; actual battles still include limited randomness and casualties over up to three rounds.

Round economy

Income depends on territory and efficiency. Smaller countries receive catch-up income. Army and armor create upkeep costs. Recruitment depends on territory, logistics, technology, and an army catch-up factor. Armies above their sustainable supply limit can lose troops to attrition.
