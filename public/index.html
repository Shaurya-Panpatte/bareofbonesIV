GAME GUIDE

How every stat and upgrade works

Every player receives one unique national doctrine

Doctrines are shuffled when the game starts and assigned without replacement, so no two players in the same match receive the same starting buff. Each doctrine has a visible strength and a visible trade-off; player order does not choose the doctrine.

Unique starting doctrines

Mass Mobilization

Starts with 142 troops. It sacrifices efficiency, armor, and money for immediate military pressure.

+26 troops · −6 efficiency · −1 armor · −$60

Industrial Engine

Starts with 100 efficiency and $760. Its income and combat efficiency are stronger, but its opening army and armor are smaller.

+14 efficiency · +$80 · −8 troops · −1 armor

Logistics Network

Starts at logistics level 3, giving noticeably stronger attack coordination, local defense response, recruitment, supply capacity, and upkeep savings.

Logistics Lv.3 · −8 troops · −2 efficiency · −1 armor

Fortress State

Starts at fortification level 3 for a strong permanent defensive bonus, but begins with fewer troops and weaker efficiency.

Fortification Lv.3 · −12 troops · −4 efficiency · −$30

Armored Command

Starts with 6 armor, creating a major defensive and casualty-reduction advantage until the armor is destroyed.

6 armor · −8 troops · −4 efficiency · −$40

Technical Directorate

Starts with technology level 3 and logistics level 2, creating a flexible combined-arms advantage at the cost of opening troop count.

Technology Lv.3 · Logistics Lv.2 · +2 efficiency · −10 troops

Upgrade shop

Army

Adds troops immediately. The first purchase adds 24 troops; later purchases give 2 fewer each time, down to a minimum of 12. Large armies cost more upkeep and can suffer supply attrition.

Starting cost: $140 · Cost rises by $80 per purchase

Efficiency

Adds 5 efficiency, up to 120. Every point above or below 85 changes combat strength by 0.45%. Efficiency also changes gross territory income through a visible income multiplier.

Starting cost: $170 · Cost rises by $90 per purchase

Technology

Adds 1 technology level, up to level 10. Every level above 2 adds 3.5% combat strength and also improves automatic recruitment each round.

Starting cost: $260 · Cost rises by $165 per purchase

Armor

Adds 1 armored vehicle. Every surviving armor unit adds +3% defense, capped at +18%, and reduces defending casualties by 1.8%, capped at 12%. Armor costs $7 upkeep per round and can be destroyed in the interception minigame.

Starting cost: $220 · Cost rises by $125 per purchase

Logistics

Adds 1 level, up to level 8. Each level above 1 adds +3% attack strength, +2 percentage points to the local defending force, +4 recruits per round, +40 supply capacity, and an 8% army-upkeep discount, capped at 35%.

Starting cost: $200 · Cost rises by $135 per purchase

Fortification

Adds 1 level, up to level 8. Each level above 1 adds +3% defense to every territory you currently defend.

Starting cost: $170 · Cost rises by $100 per purchase

Combat modifiers

MoraleEach point above or below 100 changes combat strength by 0.3%. Morale rises after victories and falls after defeats.
War exhaustionEach point reduces combat strength by 0.15%. It rises after fighting and falls by 8 each new round.
Friendly supportEach friendly tile adjacent to the attacking source tile adds 1.2% attack strength.
Home defenseDefenders receive a fixed +3% bonus.
CapitalDefending your original capital adds +8% defense.
Local defense forceA territory uses 22% of the defender’s total army, plus 2 percentage points for every logistics level above 1, capped at 34%.

The displayed estimate combines committed troops, the defender’s local force, stats, terrain, armor, fortification, morale, exhaustion, support, logistics, and capital status. Estimates are capped between 15% and 85%; actual battles still include limited randomness and casualties over up to three rounds.

Disconnects, forfeits, draws, and resignations

Temporary disconnectAny pending battle involving the disconnected player is cancelled and their turn is skipped. Their exact owned tiles are saved for 2 minutes, while the visible territory is divided as evenly as possible among active connected countries sharing a direct border.
Automatic reconnectionThis browser stores a private match-recovery token. Reopening the game in the same browser within 2 minutes automatically attempts to rejoin the room. A recovery banner also lets you retry manually.
Territory restorationSuccessful reconnection returns the exact saved tile coordinates to the player, even if those tiles were temporarily held or captured by another country during the absence. The reconnected player waits until their next normal turn.
Recovery expiryIf the player does not reconnect within 2 minutes, they permanently forfeit. Temporary holders keep the land. If no active neighbour could receive a tile, it stays under the disconnected country until it can be reassigned or captured.
DrawA draw only succeeds when every active connected player accepts it. A player may withdraw their vote before unanimity is reached.
ResignResigning still eliminates the player immediately and has no reconnection window.

Round economy

Gross territory income is multiplied by efficiency. Smaller countries receive catch-up income. Logistics discounts army upkeep, adds four recruits per level, and increases sustainable supply by 40 troops per level. Armor still costs $7 per unit. Armies above their sustainable supply limit can lose troops to attrition.
